# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in NIBI to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect privacy: do not publish other people's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or repeated disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and any project space where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to `contact@nibistudio.top` with the subject `[CONDUCT]`.
Maintainers will review reports confidentially and may remove content, warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participation.
